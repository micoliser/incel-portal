## SUMMARY

The Incel Workspace Portal is a centralized digital workspace designed to streamline work management, application access, and organizational operations. It brings everything you need into one secure, unified platform for managing tasks, collaborating on projects, and maintaining control over internal applications.

### KEY BENEFITS

- Centralized task and project management
- Secure access control for internal applications
- Real-time notifications and activity tracking
- Flexible task assignment with recurring schedule support
- Complete audit trail for compliance and accountability
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Intuitive, modern user interface

## WHAT IS THE INCEL WORKSPACE PORTAL?

The portal is an enterprise-grade web application serving as the central hub for:

1. **Task management:** Work is organized through a task system allowing you to create, assign, track, and complete work items. Each task has a description, priority level, and deadline.
2. **Application management:** The portal controls access to internal applications and tools. Users can view which applications they have access to and launch them directly.
3. **Recurring task scheduling:** For recurring work that happens regularly (daily/weekly), the system automatically generates task instances according to your defined schedule.
4. **User and access control:** Administrators manage users, departments, roles, and granular access permissions throughout the organization.
5. **Inventory management:** Administrators can catalog company assets and assign them to specific users. Users can view their assigned physical assets.
6. **Audit and compliance:** Every action is logged, creating tasks, changing access, and modifying users, providing a complete audit trail for compliance requirements.
7. **Notifications and collaboration:** Users receive notifications when tasks are assigned, completed, or updated. Comments and activity history keep everyone informed.

## GETTING STARTED

### STEP 1: SETTING UP YOUR ACCOUNT AND SIGNING IN

**Your first sign-in**

1. When an administrator creates your account, you receive an email,
   "Welcome to Incel Portal - Account Created", with a **Set your password**
   button.
2. Click it, choose a password (at least 8 characters, not a common
   password, not similar to your email), and confirm it.
3. The link works once and expires after 72 hours. If it has expired, use
   "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page to get a new one.

There is no default password. Nobody else, including IT, knows your password.

**Signing in**

1. Open https://workspace.incelgroup.com
2. Enter your work email address and your password
3. Click "Sign in to Workspace"

After a wrong password the page tells you how many attempts you have left.
After 10 wrong attempts, sign-in from your device is blocked for 15 minutes.

**Forgot your password?**

Click "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page and enter your email. If an
account exists for it, you will receive a reset link valid for one hour. At
most three reset emails are sent per hour.

If you do not have an account, ask the IT team to create one for you.

### STEP 2: UNDERSTANDING YOUR DASHBOARD

Upon login, you will see:

- Your assigned tasks (created by others, assigned to you)
- Your created tasks (assigned to team members)
- Quick action buttons to major sections
- Recent activity and snapshot panels

### STEP 3: EXPLORE NAVIGATION

The sidebar contains links to:

- Dashboard - Your personalized home screen
- Tasks - View and manage all tasks
- To-dos - Your personal to-do list with reminders
- Goals - Track your performance targets
- Reports & Summaries - Submit daily reports and view weekly metrics
- Org Summary - View organization-wide performance (executives)
- Applications - Browse available applications
- Support - Request IT assistance and track your tickets
- My Assets - View the physical assets and inventory assigned to you
- Inventory - Manage company assets and process assignments/returns (IT department and administrators; executives can view)
- Users - Manage user accounts (administrators; executives can view)
- Departments - The organization structure: departments, units, teams and their leaders (administrators manage; executives can view)
- Logs - View the audit trail (administrators and executives)

### STEP 4: UPDATE YOUR PASSWORD (Recommended)

1. Click "Change Password" in the header
2. Enter current password, then new password (minimum 8 characters)
3. Confirm new password and click "Change Password"

You stay signed in on the device you used; you are signed out everywhere else.

### STEP 5: CUSTOMIZE YOUR THEME

Click the sun/moon icon in the top right to switch between light and dark themes.

### TASKS MANAGEMENT

The Tasks section is where you create, manage, and track work.

**Viewing Tasks:** The task list can be filtered by:

- VIEW: Assigned to Me | Created by Me | Team (managers) | Recurring | Drafts
- STATUS: Pending, In Progress, Under Review, Completed
- PRIORITY: Low, Medium, High

**Creating a Task:**

1. Click "Create Task" button
2. Fill in:
   - Title (required)
   - Description (recommended)
   - Assigned To (required; choose several people to give each their own copy of the task, up to 50)
   - Priority level
   - Deadline (required, cannot be in the past)
3. Click "Create Task" - Assignee receives email notification

You cannot assign a task to yourself or to a disabled account. Once a task
exists, its assignee cannot be changed, and tasks cannot be deleted.

**Task workflow:**

1. The assignee starts the work (Pending -> In Progress).
2. When done, the assignee submits it for review (In Progress -> Under Review).
3. The person who assigned it either **approves** it (Completed) or
   **rejects** it with a reason, which sends it back.

Only the assigner can edit the title, description, priority and deadline.
Completed tasks are locked. Managers can see and comment on their team's
tasks but cannot change their status.

**Task Details Page:**

- Task title, status, priority, deadline
- Full description and assignee information
- Change Status (assignee moves work forward; assigner approves or rejects)
- Add Comments for collaboration (up to 500 characters)
- Attach Files to comments (up to 5MB per file)
- View complete Activity History

**Task Comments:**

Both creator and assignee can add comments to collaborate. Type your message, optionally attach a file, and click "Post Comment."

**Task Attachments:**

Supported types: Images (PNG, JPG, WebP), PDF, Text, Word and Excel documents, ZIP files

- In the comment box, attach file(s) from your computer
- Files are uploaded when you post the comment
- Files become available to task participants
- You can attach up to 10 files per comment, each up to 5MB
- You can also add up to 10 links to a comment

### RECURRING TASKS

For work that repeats on a schedule, the system automatically creates task instances according to your defined schedule.

**Creating a Recurring Task Schedule:**

1. Click on "Create Task"
2. Change the view to "Recurring task"
3. Define:
   - Basic Info: Title, Description, Assign To, Priority
   - Scheduling:
     - Frequency: Daily or Weekly
     - Interval: Every X days/week
     - Weekdays (if weekly): Select Mon, Tue, Wed, etc.
     - Times: What time should tasks be created? (e.g., 09:00, 17:00)
     - Time zone: Nigerian time (the default) or Dubai time. Run times are in the chosen time zone
     - Start Date: When does this begin?
     - End Date: When should this stop? (optional for indefinite)
4. Click "Create Recurring Schedule" - Assignee notified

**Managing Recurring Schedules:**

For each schedule you can:

- VIEW DETAILS: See all schedule information and next run date
- EDIT SCHEDULE: Update title, description, frequency, times, dates - Assignee receives notification
- PAUSE SCHEDULE: Temporarily stop automatic task creation, can resume later
- RESUME SCHEDULE: Restart automatic task creation after pause
- END SCHEDULE: Permanently stop the recurring task (cannot be undone)

**Automatic Task Creation:**

When a recurring schedule is active at the scheduled time:

- System automatically creates a task instance
- Task is assigned to the designated person
- Email notification sent
- Task appears in "Assigned to Me" view
- Schedule's "Next Run" date updates

### INVENTORY & ASSET MANAGEMENT

Manage company physical assets and assignments.

Who can do what: the IT department and administrators manage assets;
executives can view and export them; everyone can see the assets assigned
to them under My Assets.

**Viewing Inventory (IT and administrators):**

- Go to the Inventory section
- View all company assets across different categories
- Filter assets by status (e.g. available, assigned, maintenance), category, or managing department
- Search for items by name, serial number, or assignee name

**Managing Inventory (IT and administrators):**

- **Create/Edit Items**: Add or edit inventory items with smart edit detection (aborts if no changes), attach a serial number, category, purchase date, and upload photos.
- **Assign Items**: Open an item's details and click "Assign" to link the physical asset to a specific user.
- **Assign to a department**: an item can also be assigned to a whole department instead of a person.
- **Process Returns**: When a user returns an item, click "Process Return" to mark it as available again. Condition notes can be added upon return.
- **Maintenance**: log repairs with up to 5 attachments; the item shows as "In maintenance" until the log is completed. Retired items cannot go into maintenance.
- **Retire instead of delete**: items that have ever been assigned or serviced cannot be deleted, to keep their history. Mark them as retired.
- **Photos**: PNG, JPG or WebP only.

**Viewing My Assets (All Users):**

- Click on "My Assets" in the sidebar
- View a dedicated list of all physical assets (laptops, phones, etc.) assigned to you
- Review assignment dates and item details

### REPORTS & SUMMARIES

Maintain a record of your daily work and weekly performance.

**Daily Reports:**
- Go to Reports to submit your daily activities
- Add multiple entries to break down different pieces of work
- You can only add entries and comments on the current day (Nigeria time)
- Colleagues in your department can see your reports under All Reports
- Executives can comment on your entries
- Forward a report by email to up to 5 colleagues
- When you write your first entry of the day, your manager gets an email
- At 5:05 PM (Nigeria time) on weekdays, a copy of the day's report is emailed to you and to your manager (your team lead, supervisor or line manager). Nothing is sent on days you did not write a report

**Weekly Summaries:**
- Automatically generated every Monday for the week just ended, and emailed to you at 8 AM (Nigeria time)
- Two parts, each shown only if you had activity that week:
  - **Own work** (tasks assigned to you): tasks completed out of those on your list that week, completion rate, on-time rate, high-priority tasks, comments and files
  - **Work you delegated** (tasks you gave to other people): tasks given out and to how many people, how many were completed and on time, reviews you did (approved or sent back), your average review time, and what was still waiting for your review or overdue at the end of the week
- Whichever part had more activity comes first, so someone who mostly assigns work sees delegated work first
- Tasks you assign to yourself count as your own work
- Compare performance week-over-week
- Share your summary with a specific colleague (they get an email and a notification with their own link)
- Or create a public share link: anyone signed in to the portal with the link can view it
  - The link is shown only once, when you create it. Copy it then.
  - It expires after 30 days.
  - Creating a new link replaces the old one; "Revoke Link" stops it working immediately
- Export your summary to PDF

**Organization Summary (executives):**
- View high-level metrics aggregated across the entire workspace

### SUPPORT TICKETING

Request assistance from the IT Support team or other departments.

**Creating a Ticket:**
- Navigate to the Support section and click "New Request"
- Select a category (IT Support goes to the IT department; Other goes to HR), a priority, and provide a detailed description
- You can attach files when creating the request
- The ticket progresses through lifecycle stages (Open, Assigned, In Progress, Resolved, Closed)

**Managing Tickets:**
- Reply with comments; choose files with "Attach File" and they are sent with your reply (up to 5 files per reply, 5MB each)
- The department's line manager assigns a handler, who updates the status and marks it resolved
- When resolved, you either confirm (which closes it) or reopen it
- Unconfirmed tickets are closed automatically 7 days after being resolved
- Your own line manager can view your requests but cannot act on them

## BEST PRACTICES

### TASK CREATION

**DO:**

- Use clear, descriptive titles
- Include detailed descriptions for complex work
- Attach relevant documents and context
- Set realistic deadlines
- Use appropriate priority levels
- Assign to the right person

**DON'T:**

- Create vague tasks
- Overuse "High" priority
- Assign to wrong person as a workaround
- Create hundreds of similar tasks (use recurring schedules instead)

### RECURRING SCHEDULES

**DO:**

- Use for repetitive work
- Set realistic intervals and times
- Include helpful descriptions
- Use pause/resume rather than creating/deleting repeatedly
- Review periodically to ensure they are still needed

**DON'T:**

- Create with unrealistic frequencies
- Forget to end a schedule when no longer needed
- Use for one-time work (use regular tasks instead)

### NOTIFICATIONS AND COMMUNICATION

**DO:**

- Use task comments for work-related discussions
- Attach documents to keep everything together
- Check notifications/emails regularly

**DON'T:**

- Use comments for casual chat (A chat feature is coming soon just for that)
- Ignore notifications and deadlines
- Create duplicate tasks as reminders

### SECURITY

**DO:**

- Change your password regularly
- Log out on shared computers
- Report suspicious activity
- Keep email address current

**DON'T:**

- Share login credentials
- Use weak passwords
- Leave browser logged in on shared computers

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### Can't log in?

- Verify correct email address
- Check Caps Lock is off
- Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page to get a reset link
- "Account is temporarily locked": wait 15 minutes, then try again (or reset your password)
- "This account has been disabled": contact HR or an administrator
- Your set-up link expired: use "Forgot password?" to get a new one
- Try a different browser, or clear browser cache and cookies

### Tasks are not appearing?

- Check current filter view and status
- Try clearing all filters
- Refresh the page

### Not receiving email notifications?

- Check spam/junk folder
- Verify email address in profile
- Contact administrator

### Can't upload files?

- Verify file size is under 5MB
- Check file type is supported (images: PNG, JPG, WebP; documents: PDF, text, Word, Excel, ZIP)
- Asset photos and application logos must be PNG, JPG or WebP
- Contact administrator if issue persists

### Recurring schedule is not creating tasks?

- Verify schedule has not been paused
- Check current date has not passed end date
- Verify schedule shows as "Active"
- Check assigned user is active
- Verify you have permission

### Can't access an application?

- Verify you have been granted access
- Check role and department permissions
- Contact an administrator if you believe you should have access

### A shared summary link doesn't open?

- Public links expire after 30 days, and stop working when the owner creates a new link or revokes it
- A link shared with you personally only works when you are signed in as yourself

### "Something went wrong on our side"?

- The portal could not complete the request because of a server problem. Try again in a moment; if it keeps happening, report it to IT with the time it happened
